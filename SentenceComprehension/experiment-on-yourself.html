Experiment on yourself (30-45 minutes)

↵ Back to module homepage

First, let's do an experiment.

This experiment can be done online.  It is a sentence reading experiment and may take a little bit more time and concentration than previous experiments that you've done for this class, but I've only made a short version of the experiment, so you should be able to finish in 15-30 minutes. A real verison of this experient would probably take 30-60 minutes.

To do the experiment, go to the web experiment and follow the instructions there. (If for some reason the link does not work, please let me know.)

When you're done, enter your results here in the shared class data. Also KEEP THE EXPERIMENT SCREEN OPEN, and BE SURE TO SAVE YOUR DATA!

What do you think this experiment was about? What do you think the experiment wanted to test, and what results did it want to compare? What do you predict the results might show?

When you have finished these activities, continue to the next section of the module: "Reflection on the experiment".
