Reading Study

Thank you for joining! In this study you will read sentences one word at a time.

Here is what to do:

  1. A + sign will appear on the screen. This means a new sentence is coming.
  2. You will then see the sentence with all words hidden behind dashes (––––).
  3. Press the SPACE BAR to see the first word. On a phone, tap the screen instead.
  4. Each time you press SPACE (or tap), the next word will appear and the previous word will be hidden again.
  5. Keep going until you reach the end of the sentence.
  6. After each sentence you will answer a short question about what you just read.
  7. Please read at a comfortable, normal speed.
We will start with 2 practice sentences so you can try it out. After that, the real study begins with 20 sentences.
