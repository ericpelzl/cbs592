Lexical Decision Task

A priming paradigm demo for psycholinguistics

How it works

On each trial you will see:

Fixation cross → Prime word → Target string

Your task is to decide as quickly and accurately as possible whether the target is a real English word or a nonword.

Press F for Real Word  ·  Press J for Nonword

On touchscreens, tap the on-screen buttons instead.

There are 56 trials. A brief feedback will appear after each response.

F Word
J Nonword
